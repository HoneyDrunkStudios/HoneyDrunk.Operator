## What Changed

_One to three sentences. State the change in plain language. Name the files, fields, or structures that were modified. No jargon without context._

## Why

_Rationale and context. Reference the policy, contract, or drift finding that triggered this change. Link to the source if available._

## Impact

_Who or what is affected by this change. Specify Nodes, Sectors, workflows, or downstream consumers. Call out breaking changes explicitly._

## Evidence

_Concrete references that substantiate the change._

- **Commits**: `<sha>` — _description_
- **Pull Requests**: #_number_ — _title_
- **Catalog Diff**: _field X changed from A to B_
- **Drift Report**: _link or inline summary_

## Rollback

_How to reverse this change if it causes problems. Reference the prior state or commit._
